The short version

  • One essential cookie keeps you signed in, and one remembers your cookie choice.
  • Preferences such as your theme are stored on your device only if you allow them.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

1. What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to keep. Browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) works in a similar way but stays in your browser and isn't sent to our server. This policy covers both, and uses “cookies” for either.

2. Essential (always on)

These are strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for, so they don't need consent under the GDPR, the UK and EU ePrivacy rules, or the DPDP Act.

4. Analytics and advertising

We don't use any. There are no Google Analytics, Meta pixels, ad networks or session-recording tools on Sunny Side. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent first; nothing will be turned on by default.

5. Third-party requests

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the voice features load the LiveKit client library from jsDelivr. These requests share your IP address and browser details with those providers, as any web request does, but they don't set cookies for us. Signing in with Google or LinkedIn takes you to their site, where their own cookie policies apply.

6. Changing your mind

  • Use Cookie settings in the footer of any page, or the button above. Turning preferences off removes them from this device's storage straight away.
  • You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will stop you from signing in.
  • We ask again after 12 months, or sooner if this policy changes in a way that affects your choice.
