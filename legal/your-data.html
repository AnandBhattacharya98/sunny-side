Delete your account

In Profile Settings → Your data, open Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm. This immediately and permanently erases:

  • your profile, resume and match settings
  • every job, note, cover letter, tailored resume, interview prep and timeline entry
  • job emails we matched, hiring contacts, followed companies
  • your call log, practice-call transcripts and phone codes
  • your sign-in history and password-reset links

It can't be undone, so download a copy first if you might want it. You're signed out straight away. Preferences kept in your browser can be cleared from or your browser.

Can't sign in? Ask us

If you can't access your account, or want us to delete data about you that isn't in an account (for example, if you're a hiring contact shown to a user), email {{ email() }} with the username or email address involved. We'll confirm it's you and act within 30 days, and tell you when it's done.

Removing just part of your data

  • Inbox: clear your Gmail address and app password in Profile Settings. You can also revoke the app password in your Google account.
  • Gemini key: remove it in Profile Settings.
  • Public profile: untick “Show my profile on landing page wall” in Profile Settings.
  • Resume: delete or replace the file in Profile Settings.
  • Calls API key: revoke it on the Calls page.

Data held by providers

When you delete your account, we no longer send anything about you to the providers listed in the Privacy Policy. Requests already processed by AI and voice providers are handled under their own retention terms, which for API use are typically short and not used for training.
